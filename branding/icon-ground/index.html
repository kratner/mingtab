MingTab icon grounds

Eight backgrounds for the 明 icon, at real size. The first two panels in each row are browser tab strips, dark then light: that's where your screenshot came from. Then the pinned toolbar button, and the icon next to its Four Ounces siblings, Agent Workspace (indigo) and Social Hub (jade).

Every dark background nearly vanishes in Chrome's dark tab strip: its contrast with the tab is only 1.1 to 1.6, so you see a gold glyph on a smudge rather than a tile. Options F, G and H change that: a lighter tile, a seal-red tile, or a gold edge.
