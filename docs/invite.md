# MingTab invitation (SMS + email)

Copy-paste texts for inviting someone to install MingTab. The link points at
`releases/latest`, so it stays right after each release. Sync isn't offered: the released
zips sync only to Keith's own account (see README → Sign in to sync).

## SMS

> Hey, I built a Chrome extension called MingTab. It saves your open tabs as collections so your tab bar stays clear. Free, and nothing leaves your computer. It's for desktop Chrome, so open this on your computer: https://github.com/kratner/mingtab/releases/latest (setup takes about 2 minutes; I'll email you the steps)

Shorter, if the email goes first:

> Sent you an email about MingTab, the tab manager I built. Open it on your computer when you have 2 minutes.

## Email

**Subject:** Try MingTab, the tab manager I built

Hi [name],

I built a Chrome extension called MingTab, and I'd like you to try it.

If you keep dozens of tabs open "for later", MingTab is for that. Save a whole window as a
collection, sort collections into spaces, and open them again when you need them. Your
tabs stay saved, and your tab bar stays clear. It's free, and everything stays on your own
computer.

It isn't in the Chrome Web Store, so setup is a few clicks by hand, about two minutes.
Do this on your computer (it doesn't work on phones):

1. Download the zip from https://github.com/kratner/mingtab/releases/latest
   (the file named mingtab-<version>.zip, under "Assets").
2. Unzip it into a folder you'll keep, such as Documents/MingTab. Don't delete the folder
   afterwards: Chrome runs MingTab from it.
3. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions (type it in the address bar).
4. Turn on "Developer mode" at the top right.
5. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the folder you unzipped.
6. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin MingTab.

Then open a new tab to see your board, or click the 明 icon for the side panel.

Good to know:
- Chrome may warn about "developer mode extensions". That's normal for extensions
  installed this way. Click away the warning; MingTab keeps working.
- If you use Toby or another extension that takes over the new-tab page, turn that one
  off in chrome://extensions so MingTab can show its board.
- When there's a new version, MingTab shows a notice with a Download button. Unzip it over
  the same folder and click Restart MingTab. Your saved tabs stay.
- Back up from Settings (⚙) → Download backup. Removing the extension deletes its data.

It also works in Edge and Brave, from their own extensions page.

Tell me what you think, and anything that's confusing.

Keith
